Our story

Above the city, around the table

Encima means "above" in Spanish, and that is where you will find us: on the 23rd floor of City Garden Hotel Makati, with the skyline at your table.

Our kitchen cooks Mexican- and Spanish-inspired plates alongside pasta, pizza and steaks, made for sharing. Pair them with wine, a signature cocktail or a good cup of coffee.

Open every day from morning until midnight.
